# Vendor Review Scoring Methodology

## Overview

The vendor risk score is a weighted composite of eight assessment dimensions. Each dimension is scored 1–5. The overall score is a weighted average, also expressed 1–5.

## Dimensions and Weights

| Dimension              | Weight | Description                                                        |
|------------------------|--------|--------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Security Posture       | 20%    | Published security practices, certifications, vulnerability mgmt   |
| Privacy Practices      | 15%    | Privacy policy quality, data handling transparency, GDPR/CCPA      |
| Compliance             | 15%    | SOC 2/3, ISO 27001, HIPAA, PCI DSS, FedRAMP, other frameworks     |
| Breach History         | 15%    | Public breach record, incident response track record               |
| Financial Health       | 10%    | Revenue stability, funding, profitability, going-concern risk      |
| Reputational Risk      | 10%    | D&B rating, BBB, lawsuits, regulatory actions, media coverage      |
| Leadership Stability   | 5%     | Executive turnover, key-person risk, governance structure           |
| Transparency           | 10%    | Openness about practices, public reporting, third-party audits     |

## Scoring Rubric

### 1 — Critical Risk
- Significant gaps or red flags
- Active unresolved breaches or regulatory actions
- No visible security or privacy program
- Financial distress or going-concern warnings

### 2 — High Risk
- Material weaknesses identified
- History of breaches with unclear remediation
- Minimal certifications or compliance evidence
- Declining financial trajectory
- Multiple negative news items or lawsuits

### 3 — Moderate Risk
- Baseline practices in place but incomplete
- Some certifications but gaps in coverage
- Minor past incidents, adequately addressed
- Stable but unremarkable financials
- Mixed public reputation

### 4 — Low Risk
- Strong security and privacy programs
- Current SOC 2 Type II or equivalent
- No recent breaches; clear incident response plan
- Healthy financials with growth trajectory
- Positive industry reputation

### 5 — Minimal Risk
- Industry-leading security and privacy practices
- Multiple current certifications (SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001, etc.)
- Clean breach history with proactive disclosure program
- Strong financial position with diversified revenue
- Excellent reputation, recognized by analysts

## Overall Risk Rating

| Score Range | Rating          | Recommendation                                      |
|-------------|-----------------|-----------------------------------------------------|
| 4.5 – 5.0   | Minimal Risk   | Approve — minimal conditions                        |
| 3.5 – 4.4   | Low Risk       | Approve — standard contract terms                   |
| 2.5 – 3.4   | Moderate Risk  | Conditional — require remediation plan or mitigations|
| 1.5 – 2.4   | High Risk      | Discourage — significant mitigations required        |
| 1.0 – 1.4   | Critical Risk  | Do Not Approve — unacceptable risk posture          |

## Scoring Notes

- If a dimension cannot be assessed (e.g., private company financials), note it as "Unable to Assess" and exclude from the weighted average. Adjust remaining weights proportionally.
- Always cite the evidence behind each score. A score without a citation is an opinion, not an assessment.
- When multiple signals conflict (e.g., strong certifications but recent breach), score conservatively and explain the tension.
- For private companies, Financial Health is harder to assess — use available signals: funding rounds, employee count trends, customer logos, Glassdoor reviews for stability signals.
